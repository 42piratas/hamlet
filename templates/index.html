HAMLET

Hark!

'Twill seem strange to thee, but there was a day we had nor LLMs, nor GPTs, nor Claudes.

This humble engine was wrought first in the year 2016, and now refined by such pre-AI arts as TextBlob for sentiment, TF–IDF for relevance, the Porter stemmer for shared roots, and WordNet for kindred meanings — well-known in their hour, though hardly noble.

Its purpose is plain: speak thy mind, and Hamlet shall return thee a line of the Bard that shareth thy words and matcheth thy mood. No fancy, no feigning — but an honest search through the corpus of Shakespeare.
