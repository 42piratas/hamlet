HAMLET

Hark!

'Twill seem strange to thee, but there was a day we had nor LLMs, nor GPTs, nor Claudes.

This humble engine was wrought back then, in the year 2016, by means of TextBlob — a Python library that did parse sentiment and word; well-known in its hour, though hardly noble.

Its purpose was plain: speak thy mind, and Hamlet shall return thee a line of the Bard that shareth thy words and matcheth thy mood. No fancy, no feigning — but an honest search through the corpus of Shakespeare.
